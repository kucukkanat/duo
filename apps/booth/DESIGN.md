# Fold Booth: design spec
1. **Palette:** velvet `#3a0610` / `#6e0d1c` / `#9c1a2b` (crimson highlight), brass `#c9a24a` / `#f3dc8e` / `#7a5a1c`, chrome `#f4f4f2` / `#9da1a6` / `#4b4e53`, paper `#f6f0e1`, ink `#16121a`, bulb glow `#ffd27a`, flash `#ffffff`, holo foil `#ff9ad5` / `#9be7ff` / `#c8ffb0` / `#fff3a1`.
2. **Type:** "Anton" (condensed caps) for LEFTY / RIGHTY and the marquee, "Playfair Display" italic for captions ("which twin are you?"), and "DM Mono" for strip stamps and counters. All load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks (Impact / Georgia / ui-monospace).
3. **Textures:** vertical velvet folds (layered repeating gradients plus a sheen) and a brushed-brass bezel (a conic plus linear gradient with an inner bevel shadow). Film grain covers the whole UI at 6% (animated SVG turbulence, stepped at 12fps). The paper has a fibre-noise tint.
4. **Styles:** B&W is silver gelatin (an S-curve, cool blacks and a vignette). Warm Film is Portra (lifted blacks, orange light leak, dust specks and hairline scratches). Comic uses crisp 45° halftone dots at a 7px cell, 4-tone posterize, Sobel ink edges and a cream paper ground. Holo uses an iridescent foil band in overlay and screen modes; its angle follows device tilt, with pointer and time as fallbacks.
5. **Cover:** curtains are closed with a brass-bulb marquee sign reading "Step inside". On tap, the curtain parts (900ms, the drapes ease out with a skew) and a brass-bezel viewfinder sits under velvet swags. Style chips, the chrome shutter and 4 bulbs (frames taken) sit below.
6. **Shoot:** countdown 3-2-1 (700ms per tick, then 550ms for frames 2–4) with a beep at 880 Hz and 1320 Hz on 1. Flash: a white bloom ramps to 100% in 40ms, the capture happens at 90ms, then it fades over 600ms (ease-out). One bulb lights per frame. After 4, "Unfold to develop ✦" bobs, and the fold pill pulses on non-Duo phones.
7. **Unfold storyboard (ms), primary spread 890×626 with a vertical spine. Left page: print card + strip at the spine. Right page: twins side by side:** at 0 the crease line flares brass-white and a velvet "whoomp" sounds. From 0 to 450 the right page swings open from the crease (rotateY −95°→0, perspective 1400). From 300 to 1700 the strip feeds out of the dark slot at the crease in 5 jerky printer steps with a paper-feed rattle. The strip has a slight curl (skewY plus a gradient highlight) and a soft drop shadow.
8. **Develop (1700–4200):** the strip goes from blank paper to a cyan ghost (hue-rotated, low contrast), then to rising contrast and sepia, then to the final image. A wet gloss sweep passes once. From 2600 to 3400 LEFTY and RIGHTY unfold from their mirror axis (scaleX 0→1), staggered by 180ms. At 3600 the italic caption fades in with a chime arpeggio, and at 4200 the actions slide up.
9. **Twins page:** an editorial magazine spread on paper. A kicker reads "ISSUE Nº 4 · MIRROR TWINS". LEFTY and RIGHTY are set in huge Anton type, overlapping each portrait's edge, with a thin rule and a folio, plus the italic question. Tapping a twin stamps "IT'S ME" on it.
10. **Share PNG (1080×1920):** a poster on a velvet background with a brass marquee header "FOLD BOOTH". The strip sits on the left, tilted −3°, with a curl shadow. On the right are two twin portraits in white borders with giant LEFTY / RIGHTY type, the italic "which twin are you?" and a grain overlay. The footer reads "Duo Lab · Fold Booth" in DM Mono.
