# Duo Lab hub: "The Booklet" design spec

1. **Palette.** The cover cloth is ink `#121628`, deepening to `#0a0d1a`. Foil runs from `#9c7a3c` to `#f3d79a` to `#b38a45` to `#fbe7b5`. Pages are paper `#f3ecdf`, with `#e6dccb` toward the gutter. Text on paper is ink `#1b1a24`, muted text is `#6d6457`, and rules are oxblood `#8e2b2b`. Each app has an accent: Booth `#e2583e`, Sealed `#b3262f`, Hockey `#27b59a`, Liquid `#8b6bff`.
2. **Type.** Headlines, the title and the numerals use *Fraunces* (Google Fonts, an optical serif; 9–144 opsz, 300–900 weight, italic). Eyebrows, labels and UI use *Space Grotesk* (500/700), tracked +0.18em in caps. If the fonts don't load, the fallback is `ui-serif` / New York, then `system-ui`.
3. **Materials.** The cover is cloth-bound ink with SVG `feTurbulence` grain and a woven crosshatch at about 6% opacity. A debossed double rule sits 14px from the edge as an inset highlight over a shadow. The title is foil-stamped: a gradient that `background-clip`s the text, with a specular band whose position follows device tilt (or the pointer on desktop). The spine at the centre is a stitched dashed line. Pages carry paper grain, a soft gutter shadow and a gold thread in the gutter.
4. **Closed layout (cover screen).** It runs top to bottom: eyebrow, the big foil title, the italic subtitle, then the emblem with "Open me" breathing (scale 1→1.04, 2.8s). Below that is a debossed **contents list** with 01–04 rows (thumb zone, rows at least 52px). The foot holds the foil "Unfold" pill, the unfold counter and "How it works".
5. **Open layout (inner screen).** A magazine spread over two pages. Each page has a running head and two cards. Each card has a huge Fraunces numeral (01–04) in the app's tint and a living plate with a mini preview, then the name, the hook and an italic "Unfold →" teaser. The folio and the counter sit at the bottom.
6. **Living previews.** Booth: a photostrip slides from a slot while the flash blinks. Sealed: the wax seal rocks, cracks, and the letter peeks out. Hockey: a puck bounces with a neon trail between two paddles across a dashed crease. Liquid: gooey blobs wobble (blur plus contrast).
7. **Storyboard: unfold (closed→open, 1400ms).** **0ms:** the cover is cut at the crease into two 3D doors. Each has a front (the cover), a back (marbled endpaper) and a page-block edge face at the crease, all with `perspective: 1700px`. **0–120ms:** a gold seam of light appears along the crease, and a haptic plus a paper "fwip" play. **80–1050ms:** the doors swing toward you, the left one `rotateY(0→-168deg)` around the left edge and the right mirrored (cubic-bezier .2,.75,.15,1). The fronts darken as they turn. **120–900ms:** a contact shadow under each door slides outward and fades, and the spread brightens from 45% to 100% and scales .965→1. **450–1250ms:** cards rise 14px and fade in, staggered 90ms (01, 03, 02, 04, so across then down). **700–1400ms:** the numerals sweep their foil once and the counter ticks up with a bump.
8. **Storyboard: fold (open→closed, 700ms).** The doors start at -150deg and swing shut with ease-in (accelerating). At 0deg the cover thumps (scale 1.012→1, 160ms), with a haptic.
9. **Gesture.** On the cover, a horizontal drag pulls the doors apart in proportion to the drag (up to 80deg). Release past 35% and the book opens; otherwise it springs closed.
10. **Launch.** Tapping a card starts a same-document View Transition. The card's plate grows to fill the screen in the app's tint, and then the page navigates. With reduced motion, every 3D move becomes a 200ms crossfade.
